# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Larger symbols on the striped banners, which have no edge trim to clear.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Every vanilla banner can carry an emblem, including banners already built in your world.
- Interact to set an emblem by typing a symbol number, e.g. `42`. The banner previews it while you
  type; Enter saves it, Escape keeps the old one.
- 196 somewhat familiar symbols, embroidered over the vanilla banner's design in white, gold
  or black depending on the banner. Striped banners, which have no edge trim, get a larger symbol.
- An empty emblem shows the plain vanilla banner.
