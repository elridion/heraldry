# Changelog

## 0.3.5

- Players on 0.3.x can still play together.

## 0.3.4

- The banner texts are translated into every language Valheim offers except Abenaki, and follow
  the game's language setting.
- Players on 0.3.x can still play together.

## 0.3.3

- Bug reports and feedback now go to the #heraldry channel on Discord.
- Players on 0.3.x can still play together.

## 0.3.2

- The symbol is larger on the red, yellow, purple and orange banners, whose trim leaves more room;
  every trimmed banner now keeps the same gap between symbol and trim.
- Players on 0.3.x can still play together.

## 0.3.1

- The gold symbol on the blue and green banners now matches each banner's gold embroidery instead
  of looking olive green, and is a little smaller so it clears the scrollwork.
- On the striped banners the symbol is counterchanged: in the stripe colour over the white, and in
  the banner's white over the stripes, so it reads across the whole banner.
- Players on 0.3.0 and 0.3.1 can still play together; only the look of the symbols differs.

## 0.3.0

- 22 new symbols, numbered 138 to 159: gems (diamond ring, cut diamond, topaz), a sun cross, a
  stone golem, a troll, the Elder, a swamp frog, pairs of ravens, dragons, sea serpents, horses,
  goats, axes, griffins and salmon, a barrel, a mushroom, an amphora, laurels, spades and a hood.
  Symbols 0 to 137 are unchanged.
- The mod page shows every symbol with its number.
- Players on 0.2.x and 0.3.0 can't play together, so everyone sees the same symbols.

## 0.2.3

- Symbol 84 is now a bridge instead of the black hand shield. Banners already set to 84 show the
  bridge.
- New mod icon: a gold banner on a crimson plate.

## 0.2.2

- Fixed banner cloth turning magenta in the falling pieces when a banner with a symbol is torn
  down. The pieces now show the plain banner cloth.

## 0.2.1

- New mod icon showing one of the new symbols.

## 0.2.0

- New symbol set: 138 heraldic symbols (beasts, skulls, crowns, castles, helms, weapons, shields,
  heraldic charges, plants, stars, ships and more) from game-icons.net, numbered 0 to 137.
- The symbol numbers now mean different symbols. Banners you already set show the new symbol with
  the same number, and numbers above 137 show a plain banner; set them again to pick a new symbol.
- Black symbols are a dark charcoal instead of near-black.
- Symbols match the resolution of the banner cloth, so they no longer look sharper than the cloth.
- Players on 0.1.x and 0.2.0 can't play together, since the same number would show different
  symbols.

## 0.1.1

- Larger symbols on the striped banners, which have no edge trim to clear.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Every vanilla banner can carry an emblem, including banners already built in your world.
- Interact to set an emblem by typing a symbol number, e.g. `42`. The banner previews it while you
  type; Enter saves it, Escape keeps the old one.
- 196 somewhat familiar symbols, embroidered over the vanilla banner's design in white, gold
  or black depending on the banner. Striped banners, which have no edge trim, get a larger symbol.
- An empty emblem shows the plain vanilla banner.
